---

## 📋 Tutorial Sections

### 1. 🎯 What This Project Does

WhatsApp Analyzer takes your raw exported WhatsApp chat files (which are usually huge and hard to read) and turns them into a beautiful, interactive dashboard. 
It securely parses every message to provide you with:
- **Instant Analytics**: Who sends the most messages? When are you most active? What emojis do you use the most?
- **AI Chat Agent**: Ask any question about your chat history (e.g., "When did we first discuss the project?") and get a summarized, factual response powered by an intelligent AI assistant.

---

### 2. 📱 How to Export Your WhatsApp Chat

Before using the analyzer, you need a `.txt` file containing your chat history.

**If you use an iPhone (iOS):**
1. Open the WhatsApp chat you want to analyze.
2. Tap on the contact's name or group name at the top.
3. Scroll down to the bottom and tap **Export Chat**.
4. Choose **Without Media** (the analyzer only reads text, not photos/videos).
5. Save the `.txt` file to your files, email it to yourself, or AirDrop it to your computer.

**If you use an Android:**
1. Open the WhatsApp chat.
2. Tap the three dots (More options) in the top right corner.
3. Tap **More** > **Export chat**.
4. Choose **Without media**.
5. Save or email the resulting `.txt` file to your computer.

---

### 3. 🌐 Using the Live Application

You don't need to install anything! The app is completely live on the internet.

1. Navigate to the Live Frontend: [https://whatsapp-analyzer-frontend.onrender.com/](https://whatsapp-analyzer-frontend.onrender.com/)
2. **Create an Account:** Click "Register" and create a secure account. (Your data is completely private and requires a login).
3. **Log In:** Use your new credentials to enter the dashboard.

---

### 4. 📈 Uploading and Analyzing

1. On the **Dashboard**, you will see an upload area.
2. Drag and drop your `.txt` WhatsApp export file into the box, or click the box to select the file from your computer.
3. A progress bar will appear. Behind the scenes, the server is securely parsing every message, timestamp, and user.
4. Once completed, your chat will appear as a "Session" card. Click on it to open the **Session View**.

---

### 5. 🤖 Interacting with the AI and Analytics

Once you are in the Session View, you have two powerful tools at your disposal:

#### 📊 The Analytics Panel
On the right side of your screen (or by clicking the Analytics tab on mobile), you'll find auto-generated charts:
- **Activity Timeline**: Shows your message volume over time.
- **Peak Hours**: Discover what time of day your group is most active.
- **Word & Emoji Clouds**: See the most frequently used words and emojis in your chat.
- **Participant Breakdown**: A pie chart showing who talks the most.

#### 💬 The AI Chat Assistant
At the bottom of the screen, you will see a chat box. 
- You can ask natural-language questions like *"Who apologizes the most?"* or *"What did we decide about dinner?"*
- The app uses a built-in search engine to find the relevant messages and securely passes them to an advanced AI model to generate a helpful answer.
- **Switching Models:** By clicking on the top header, you can switch between different top-tier AI models (like Google Gemma or OpenAI GPT) absolutely free of charge!

---

### 6. 🔒 Managing Your Data and Privacy

You have full control over your data.
- **Delete a Chat:** On the main dashboard, simply click the trash icon next to a session. This instantly and permanently deletes the file and all parsed messages from the database.
- **Change Settings/Delete Account:** Click on your profile icon in the top right to access Settings. Here you can change your password, set a default AI model, or completely delete your account (which wipes all your sessions and data permanently).
